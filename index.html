4DCAD — quick reference

Modes

Tab toggles Edit / Explore. V toggles Full 4D / Geodesic 3D (Geodesic needs a world object: pick one in the "World" menu or Properties → "Use as world").

Edit (Blender-like)

  • MMB or RMB drag: orbit · Shift+drag: pan · Ctrl+drag / wheel: zoom · Alt+drag: rotate the view into W (Geodesic: roll)
  • Click: select (click again: select parent group) · Shift+click: multi-select · A: select all · Esc: deselect
  • G grab · R rotate · S scale, then X/Y/Z/W to constrain (R: one axis = rotate about it in 3D, two axes = that plane, e.g. R X W). Click/Enter confirm, Esc/RMB cancel.
  • Shift+D duplicate · X/Del delete · Ctrl+G group (union) · F or . focus · 1/3/7 front/right/top · 9 look along W · Shift+W reset W
  • Drag library items into the viewport (or the tree). Drag tree rows onto groups to move them, onto objects to union them, onto row edges to reorder.

Explore (game-like)

  • Click to capture the mouse. WASD move, Space/Shift up/down, wheel: speed.
  • Full 4D: Q/E move along W, Z/X turn into W, C/V turn sideways in W, R reset.
  • Geodesic: you move on the world's 3D surface; Q/E roll.

Files

Scenes: Save / Open (.4dscene.json, includes custom primitives). Library: Import / Export primitives (.4dprim.json), objects (.4dobj.json) or the whole library.
